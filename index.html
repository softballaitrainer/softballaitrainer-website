About

Softball AI Trainer was built by a softball parent who wanted objective, encouraging feedback on every rep his daughter took between lessons and practices. It's not a replacement for great coaching — it's another tool in the toolbox: biomechanical analysis on every recorded pitch and swing (see the research behind it), age-calibrated scores you can track over a season, and drill suggestions targeted at what the numbers actually show. Bring the trends to your coach and make lesson time count.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need special equipment?

Just your phone. No wearables, no sensors, no extra gear.

What age groups does it work for?

10U through 18U+. Scoring is calibrated for each age bracket, so a 10U pitcher isn't measured against 18U standards.

How do younger players (under 13) sign up?

Direct account signup is for ages 13 and up. For younger athletes, a parent or guardian creates an account and adds the child as an athlete profile through a parental consent flow designed to meet COPPA's verifiable-consent requirements. A parent can manage 1 athlete on the free tier, up to 2 on the Player plan.

Is my child's video private?

Yes. All video analysis happens on your device — the app never uploads your video or photos. AI coaching uses your athlete's age bracket, scores, skeletal position data, and the questions you type — never images or video, and never your athlete's name (the app swaps it for a placeholder before anything leaves the phone). The only images that leave your phone are analysis cards you choose to share yourself. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Is Coach Jodi safe for young athletes?

Coach Jodi is an AI coach and is labeled as one in the app. It runs behind a fixed safety prompt that keeps replies about softball and age-appropriate, defers health questions to a doctor or athletic trainer, and points anyone in distress to a trusted adult (and to 988 in the US). Every reply carries a Report flag that we review. Athletes are asked not to share personal details, the app never sends your athlete's name to the AI provider, under-13 athletes can only use Coach Jodi after a parent consents, and AI coaching can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Does it work for left-handed athletes?

Yes. Set your athlete's throwing and batting hand once, and every analysis scores accordingly — left-handed mechanics are first-class, not mirrored afterthoughts.

How is this different from other video analysis apps?

Built specifically for fastpitch softball, with age-calibrated scoring and AI coaching personalized to your mechanics.

Does it work without internet?

Video analysis runs entirely on your phone, so you can record and analyze at any field with no signal. An internet connection is only needed for Coach Jodi's AI feedback and syncing your account.

What’s included in the free tier?

The free tier currently includes 12 video analyses per week, 3 Coach Jodi questions per day, your last 10 saved sessions, and 1 athlete profile. The Player plan raises that to unlimited analyses and up to 2 athlete profiles; the Coach plan covers up to 15 athletes.

What phones does it support?

Android at launch (Google Play), with iOS to follow. Any modern Android phone works — newer phones simply analyze faster.

How accurate is the analysis?

Scoring is built on pose detection plus biomechanical metrics anchored to peer-reviewed fastpitch pitching research and established coaching standards, with age-specific targets. It’s a training aid, not a lab: accuracy depends on video quality and camera angle, and we publish our methods and limitations openly on the research page.
