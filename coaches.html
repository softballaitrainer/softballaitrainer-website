Coach Questions

How is this different from the player plan?

The Coach plan adds a roster system. You can manage up to 15 athletes from one account, with separate analysis history and drill recommendations for each player. The Player plan covers up to 2 athlete profiles (the free tier covers 1), so a roster needs the Coach plan.

What if I have more than 15 athletes?

Contact us at support@softballaitrainer.com. We’re working on larger team tiers for high school and college programs.

Do parents need their own accounts?

No. As the coach, you manage all athlete profiles under your account. If a parent wants their own dashboard for their child, they can create their own account separately (free tier: 1 athlete; Player plan: up to 2).

Is athlete data private?

Yes. All video analysis happens on your device — the app never uploads video or photos to any server. Athlete data is stored under your account and is not visible to other users; we share limited data only with the service providers listed in our Privacy Policy to operate the app. For athletes under 13, analysis stays locked until the child's parent grants consent through a secure link you send them — parental consent is built in, not bolted on.

Can I share analysis with parents?

Yes — you choose what to share. You can send an analysis card (athlete first name, scores, and a keyframe image) to a parent by text or email from your device's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you explicitly send it.

Does it work for both pitching and hitting?

Yes. Every athlete can have both pitching and hitting analyses. Coach Jodi provides skill-specific feedback for each.
